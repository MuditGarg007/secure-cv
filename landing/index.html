How It Works

Three Layers, Zero Secrets

The key never leaves your device.

Passkeys are FIDO2/WebAuthn keys locked to your phone, laptop or security key, and to this exact website. We only ever store a public key. A look-alike site gets nothing it can use: the browser refuses to sign for the wrong origin.

Your face and hand, only when it matters.

New country, new browser, repeated failures? We ask for a quick check: your own hand shape plus one random sign, held next to your face. A photo or old video can't pass, and it never signs you in on its own.

A way back in without a weak link.

Lost every device? Your recovery code plus a face check starts a cooldown. Every signed-in device is alerted and can cancel it. No SMS, no email link, no help-desk password reset.

Look-alike phishing site

Passkeys are bound to the real origin and rpID. The browser won't release them to a clone, and a relayed assertion fails the server's origin check.

SIM swap & OTP interception

There are no OTPs to intercept, swap or read out over the phone. Sign-in is a cryptographic signature from your own device.

Photo or replayed video

Every check asks for your own hand shape plus a random sign in a random order, next to the same face, inside a tight time window.

Our Mission

Passwords get reused, guessed and leaked. SMS codes get swapped and read out over the phone. Secure brings phishing-proof passkeys to Indian government, education and small-business services, with a path for people on basic phones, a voice prompt on every screen, and recovery that never falls back to something weaker.

web/Next.js · SQLite · WebAuthn

Runs the passkey ceremonies with user verification required, checks origin, rpID, challenge and signCount on every one, scores each sign-in for risk, and writes an append-only audit trail.
